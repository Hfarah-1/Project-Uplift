Heisman Winner Fernando Mendoza Commits $500K to MS Research

JA
Jake Aaron
3 min read

Fernando Mendoza, the Heisman Trophy winner and the recent number one pick in the NFL draft, has committed over $500,000 to the National MS Society. This organization supports people with multiple sclerosis, a chronic autoimmune disease where the immune system attacks nerves in the brain and spinal cord, which causes communication issues, inflammation, and scarring.

Fernando, who will play for the Las Vegas Radars this upcoming NFL season, along with his brother Alberto, who will play this upcoming college football season for Georgia Tech, started the Mendoza Family Fund in honor of their mother Elisa who has MS.

For years, the Mendoza brothers have raised over $360,000.

When Fernando was at the University of California, Berkeley, the Mendoza Burrito (rice, black beans, carne asada, chimichurri and quince sauce) was created. Through a percentage split, the burrito ended up raising 10,000 for MS research.

When Mendoza transferred to Indiana, the Mendoza Burger was created at the Buffalouie's. The burger is made of two smashed patties with ham, Swiss cheese, pickles, mustard, and grilled onions all married together between brioche buns. Buffalouie's sister restaurant also introduced a Mendoza-themed Cuban. With the restaurants matching a percentage of each sandwich sold, they have donated over $150,000 to the MS Society.

For more information, visit nationalmssociety.org.
